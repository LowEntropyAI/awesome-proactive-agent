# Card artwork

Paper cards prefer a numbered illustration from the paper. The entries in `paper-figures.json` link each local thumbnail to the exact source figure or PDF page. A PDF crop is labeled as an excerpt in the detail view. Previously curated author figures are retained where appropriate. When no suitable figure has been verified, a labeled editorial cover is used instead.

`project-marks.json` lists original project, product, and publisher mark URLs. Captions distinguish a publisher or documentation mark from a product-specific logo. The product name beside a mark is website text, not part of the original artwork.

Figures and marks remain credited to their respective authors and owners; inclusion here does not relicense them.

Choose by caption and visual content, not by figure number or the first downloadable image. For a system, show what initiates its behavior; for an evaluation, show its tasks or protocol. Inspect the complete figure in the paper, including vector illustrations embedded as HTML objects. A screenshot or results plot can be appropriate when it actually illustrates the main contribution. Credit the specific figure or PDF page.
