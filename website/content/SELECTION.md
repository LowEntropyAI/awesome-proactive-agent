# Essential Reading

The entry points cover complementary mechanisms, not a ranking or an exhaustive canon.

- Interaction models: Gander (full-duplex interaction and asynchronous task work), StreamVibe (evolving intent with a stateful streaming harness), JoyAI-VL-Interaction (respond, remain silent or delegate).
- Agent systems: Proactive Agent (event-driven task anticipation), MemoProAgent (personal memory and intervention), PASK (latent needs and assistance depth).
- Questions, preferences and boundaries: Ask Less, Ask Better (attention cost), AttriFy (inspectable preference clarification), SAFE-PRO (risk-conditioned inquiry).
- Evaluation: π-Bench (long-horizon workflows), P3I (timely video assistance), HALO (human-grounded timing and intensity).

EgoVoice and PIRA-Bench remain in the collection; their selected slots give way to a broader coverage of interaction and human-grounded evaluation. StreamVibe is an arXiv work with announced code/weights; selection does not imply artifact availability or publication acceptance.

Evidence: linked original papers/project sources and repository evidence notes. These are editorial reading routes, not new mutually exclusive paper categories.
