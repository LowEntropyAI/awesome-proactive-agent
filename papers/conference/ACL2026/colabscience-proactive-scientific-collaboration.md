# "Excuse me, may I say something..." CoLabScience, A Proactive AI Assistant for Biomedical Discovery and LLM-Expert Collaborations

## Why It Matters

CoLabScience studies an especially difficult form of proactive assistance: contributing a useful cross-literature insight to a live research discussion without interrupting experts at the wrong moment.

## Proactivity Signal

PULI separates an Observer that decides whether to intervene from a Presenter that generates the contribution, grounding both in the project proposal and long- and short-term discussion memory.

## Evaluation Setup

The BSDD benchmark contains 3,206 simulated biomedical research discussions derived from recent PubMed papers. Timing and content are evaluated with automatic metrics, model judgments, and a smaller expert review; PULI improves intervention precision and collaborative utility over the reported baselines.

## Key Limitations

Most discussions are model-generated, the expert-reviewed sample is limited, and the domain is restricted to biomedicine. Offline intervention quality does not show that the agent improves real scientific decisions.

## Use For

Use this for streaming collaboration agents, observer-presenter architectures, positive-unlabeled learning of intervention timing, and proactive scientific-assistant evaluation.
