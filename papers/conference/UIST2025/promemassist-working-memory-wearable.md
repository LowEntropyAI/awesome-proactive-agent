# ProMemAssist: Exploring Timely Proactive Assistance Through Working Memory Modeling in Multi-Modal Wearable Devices

## Why It Matters

ProMemAssist makes a user's moment-to-moment working-memory state the basis for proactive assistance, rather than relying only on task progress or predefined triggers.

## Proactivity Signal

Using camera and microphone signals from smart glasses, the system models memory items and episodes in real time, then predicts whether the benefit of assistance outweighs the likely interruption cost.

## Evaluation Setup

The authors evaluate the system with 12 participants completing cognitively demanding tasks and compare it with an LLM-based assistance baseline. ProMemAssist produced more selective assistance and higher user engagement.

## Key Limitations

The evaluation is small and task-specific. Its working-memory model depends on reliable multimodal sensing and may not transfer directly to other devices, users, or open-ended daily settings.

## Use For

Use this for cognitive-state-aware intervention timing, wearable proactive assistants, and designs that explicitly balance assistance value against interruption cost.
