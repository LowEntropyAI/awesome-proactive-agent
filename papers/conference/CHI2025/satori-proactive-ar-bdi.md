# Satori: Towards Proactive AR Assistant with Belief-Desire-Intention User Modeling

## Why It Matters

Satori combines a classical Belief-Desire-Intention user model with a multimodal LLM to provide proactive AR guidance for physical tasks. It is a useful bridge between symbolic user-state modeling, open-ended perception, and human-centered intervention timing.

## Proactivity Signal

The assistant observes the user and environment, infers current belief, desire, and intention, and presents contextually appropriate guidance without waiting for an explicit request.

## Evaluation Setup

The design is grounded in two formative studies with 12 experts and evaluated in a 16-participant within-subject study. The paper compares Satori with a designer-configured Wizard-of-Oz assistant and reports comparable task-assistance performance without domain-specific manual rules.

## Key Limitations

The user study is small and centered on bounded AR tasks. A strong Wizard-of-Oz comparison demonstrates feasibility but does not establish robustness across users, environments, long sessions, or erroneous BDI-state estimates.

## Use For

Use this for proactive AR, multimodal user modeling, BDI-guided assistance, physical-task intervention timing, and human evaluation of context-aware guidance.
