# StreamGaze: Gaze-Guided Temporal Reasoning and Proactive Understanding in Streaming Videos

[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.01707) · [Code](https://github.com/daeunni/StreamGaze)

## Why It Matters

Adds human gaze to streaming evaluation, making user attention an explicit source of context.

## Proactivity Signal

Proactive tasks test anticipation and gaze-triggered alerting using past/current frames.

## Evaluation Setup

The gaze-video QA pipeline aligns egocentric video with fixation and scanpath information. Evaluation covers past, present, and proactive tasks; the paper reports substantial model-human gaps.

## Key Limitations

This is a benchmark, not a new interaction model. Gaze is an intent cue; it does not establish user authorization or intervention utility.

## Use For

Gaze-conditioned intent inference, proactive streaming evaluation, and attention-input ablations.
