# MiniCPM-o 4.5: Towards Real-Time Full-Duplex Omni-Modal Interaction

[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.27393) · [Code](https://github.com/OpenBMB/MiniCPM-o)

## Why It Matters

Provides an omni-modal model for simultaneous perception and response.

## Proactivity Signal

The paper describes reminders and comments grounded in continuously observed live scenes.

## Evaluation Setup

Omni-Flow aligns streaming audio/video inputs and speech/text outputs on a shared temporal axis. Evaluation covers vision, speech, omni-modal understanding, and interaction efficiency.

## Key Limitations

Full-duplex I/O and useful initiative need separate evaluation. Model benchmarks alone do not establish interruption quality or autonomous GUI task success.

## Use For

Streaming omni-modal interfaces, overlapping perception/output, and scene-conditioned interaction.
