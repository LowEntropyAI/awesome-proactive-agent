# InsightToast: Proactive Information Retrieval & Glanceable Visualization in the Side Channel of Data-Rich Meetings

## Why It Matters

InsightToast is a concrete mixed-initiative design for supplying missing institutional context without forcing participants to stop a meeting and search. It combines proactive retrieval with a deliberately peripheral presentation channel.

## Proactivity Signal

The system continuously monitors verbal discourse, identifies emerging information needs, retrieves source-grounded evidence, and pushes short text or interactive charts as ephemeral side-channel toasts without a direct query.

## Evaluation Setup

The multi-agent RAG pipeline is demonstrated over legislative documents and evaluated in a comparative study with 16 participants making policy decisions. The study examines whether participants gain relevant context while maintaining natural conversational flow.

## Key Limitations

The study is small and uses a single policy-decision scenario. Continuous meeting monitoring raises privacy, consent, attribution, and false-interruption questions that are not resolved by a source-grounded retrieval pipeline alone.

## Use For

Use this for meeting copilots, peripheral proactive interfaces, multimodal information-need detection, source-grounded retrieval, and low-friction visual interventions during collaboration.
