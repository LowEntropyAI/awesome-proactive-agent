# Towards Cognitive Process-Aware Proactive Writing Support

## Why It Matters

The paper maps observable writing behavior to an interpretable cognitive-process model, offering a structured answer to what kind of help a proactive writing system should provide when the user cannot articulate a prompt.

## Proactivity Signal

AToM CoWriter infers the writer's current cognitive process from interaction traces and document context, then surfaces one of 14 associated support types as a proactive suggestion.

## Evaluation Setup

The framework is grounded in a formative study and literature review, then tested in two within-subjects studies with 21 participants. The reported evidence suggests improved expressiveness and idea exploration, with higher engagement when cognitive-process inference guides the suggestions.

## Key Limitations

The work explicitly focuses on what to suggest rather than solving when to intervene. The participant sample is small, the evidence is short-horizon, and cognitive-process labels are an interpretive model rather than direct measurements of internal state.

## Use For

Use this for proactive writing tools, interpretable need inference, support-type taxonomies, and systems that separate content selection from intervention-timing policy.
