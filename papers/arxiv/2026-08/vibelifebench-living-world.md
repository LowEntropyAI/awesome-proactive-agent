# VibeLifeBench: Can Your Life Agent Be Proactive and Persistent in a Living World?

## Why It Matters

VibeLifeBench turns long-horizon personal assistance into a changing-world problem rather than a sequence of isolated requests. It makes proactive restraint as important as initiative by requiring agents to preserve implicit constraints and authorization boundaries across weeks.

## Proactivity Signal

The agent explicitly decides when to act, when to ask for missing information or permission, and when to stay silent. Silent world mutations are not announced to the agent, so useful behavior also requires re-inspecting relevant services and adapting a persistent plan without a fresh user request describing each change.

## Evaluation Setup

The benchmark contains 200 multi-week tasks across 10 life domains, implemented over 22 mock services with 288 tool interfaces. Its scripted timelines include 7,453 events and 1,483 silent mutations, while 12,261 weighted stage checks evaluate both task progress and constraint compliance; seven frontier models are tested, with the strongest reported avg@3 score reaching 32.5.

## Key Limitations

The environment is simulated and its timelines, personas, services, and mutations are scripted. It therefore does not establish reliability, privacy, user acceptance, or consent calibration in real longitudinal deployments, and the benchmark artifacts are described as forthcoming rather than currently released.

## Use For

Use this for long-horizon life agents, act/ask/silent policies, persistent planning under hidden changes, authorization-aware tool use, and benchmarks where inaction can be the correct proactive decision.
