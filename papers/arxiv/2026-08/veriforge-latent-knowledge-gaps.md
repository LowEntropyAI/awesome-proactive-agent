# VeriForge: Mitigating Latent Knowledge Gaps in Narrative Drafting via Mixed-Initiative Scaffolding

## Why It Matters

VeriForge targets information needs that users cannot explicitly query because they do not yet know a gap exists. It separates initiative over knowledge discovery from author control over prose, offering a useful design pattern for proactive assistance that surfaces evidence without taking over the creative task.

## Proactivity Signal

As an author drafts, the system proactively highlights passages that may contain latent knowledge gaps without waiting for a search request. The author can then inspect source-anchored Knowledge Cards, ask reactive follow-up questions, and organize evidence on a spatial canvas while retaining full initiative over narrative synthesis.

## Evaluation Setup

The design is grounded in formative interviews with nine fiction writers and evaluated in a within-subject study with 12 writers against a strengthened baseline sharing the same retrieval backend. The 30-minute cold-start writing tasks, interaction logs, surveys, interviews, verification probe, and blind ratings provide preliminary evidence for better blind-spot recognition, creative exploration, and domain grounding.

## Key Limitations

The summative evidence comes from 12 mostly hobbyist or online writers completing short historical-martial-arts scenes. It does not establish sustained benefit across longer writing projects, other genres, or professional domains, and the authors note that the Knowledge Canvas can become visually dense.

## Use For

Use this for proactive knowledge-gap detection, mixed-initiative writing tools, evidence-first interventions, user-agency-preserving scaffolds, and interfaces that must reveal unknown unknowns without generating the user's final work.
