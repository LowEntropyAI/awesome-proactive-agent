# Anticipate and Learn: Unleashing Idle-Time Compute in Proactive Agents

## Why It Matters

ProAct reframes idle time as a useful compute window for proactive agents to anticipate needs, gather evidence, and reduce future user effort.

## Proactivity Signal

The agent predicts future user needs from dialogue history and memory, then prepares relevant evidence before the user asks.

## Evaluation Setup

ProActEval covers proactive assistant scenarios and measures effects such as reduced user effort, evidence readiness, and hallucination reduction.

## Key Limitations

Predicting future needs can become intrusive or wasteful without strong utility thresholds and consent-aware delivery. The paper focuses more on preparation than social interruption.

## Use For

Use this for idle-time compute, future-need prediction, evidence acquisition, and proactive memory-to-action pipelines.
