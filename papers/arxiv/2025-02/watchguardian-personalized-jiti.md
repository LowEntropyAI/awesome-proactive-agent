# WatchGuardian: Enabling User-Defined Personalized Just-in-Time Intervention on Smartwatch

## Why It Matters

WatchGuardian demonstrates a concrete, user-configurable proactive intervention system on a resource-constrained wearable. Users define which personal behaviors should trigger help, giving the intervention policy an explicit source of consent and personalization.

## Proactivity Signal

The smartwatch continuously detects a user-defined undesirable action from inertial sensing and initiates an intervention at the moment the behavior occurs, without requiring a contemporaneous request.

## Evaluation Setup

An offline study with 26 participants evaluates few-shot personalization using three, five, or ten examples. A four-hour intervention study compares WatchGuardian with a rule-based baseline; the paper reports a 64.0% average reduction in targeted actions and a 29.0-point advantage over the baseline.

## Key Limitations

The study is short, behavior classes are defined in advance, and the intervention mechanism focuses on detection rather than rich dialogue or action planning. Long-term habituation, false-alert fatigue, privacy, and on-device deployment costs remain open.

## Use For

Use this for wearable proactive agents, personalized just-in-time intervention, few-shot sensing, user-defined trigger policies, and human-centered false-positive analysis.
