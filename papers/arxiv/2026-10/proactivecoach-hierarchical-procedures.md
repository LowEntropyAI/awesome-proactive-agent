# Improving Proactive AI Assistance with Hierarchical Procedural Understanding

[Primary source](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.06505) · Checked 2026-10-08 · arXiv preprint. Review depth: abstract and arXiv metadata; no experiments rerun.

## Why It Matters

Helpful procedural coaching depends on whether a person needs phase-level, step-level, or action-level guidance at that moment.

## Proactivity Signal

ProactiveCoach uses hierarchical procedural understanding to choose guidance or silence, with a router adapting the guidance level to inferred expertise.

## Evaluation Setup

ProactiveCoach-Instruct and ProactiveCoach-Bench support the study. The abstract reports gains up to 9.6 percentage points for hierarchical rather than fixed-level guidance and evaluates adaptation across expertise-level transitions.

## Key Limitations

Task hierarchy and expertise transitions are benchmark constructions. Gains against fixed-level or in-context baselines should not be read as evidence of general user modeling or field deployment.

## Use For

Use for expertise-aware intervention granularity, procedural video assistance, and evaluating both guidance and silence.
