# Exploring Visual Access and Proactive AI Tutoring for Handwritten Mathematics

[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.26411) · [Release tracker](https://github.com/nachiketdk/proactive-ai-tutoring) · First posted 2026-10-19 · Checked 2026-10-24.

## Why It Matters

Direct proactive tutoring grounded in a learner's ongoing handwritten work, with an explicit wait option rather than automatic commentary on every change.

## Proactivity Signal

Periodic camera views and prior assistance inform three choices: repair a consequential error, support a stalled valid transition, or wait. A pause alone is not sufficient evidence that the learner needs help.

## Evaluation Setup

A randomized study with 117 adults compares text-reactive, visual-reactive and visual-proactive tutors. Analysis covers 2,140 messages, learner experience and learning outcomes; the described corpus contains 255,984 timestamped images.

## Key Limitations

A short adult calculus study does not establish sustained learning benefits or transfer to children. This is a periodically sampled multimodal application, not a new native streaming foundation model.

## Use For

Intervention restraint and grounded tutoring. Although the paper announces a release, the linked repository currently contains only a README promising later dataset availability; platform code and corpus files were not verified.
