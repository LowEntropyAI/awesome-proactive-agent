# Conversation Is a Two-Body Problem: Dyadic Evaluation of Full-Duplex Dialogue Models

[Primary source](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.08125) · Checked 2026-10-08 · arXiv preprint. Review depth: abstract and arXiv metadata; no experiments rerun.

## Why It Matters

Conversation quality emerges from both participants' timing and behavior; a single fixed interlocutor can conceal coupled failures.

## Proactivity Signal

DyaFDB runs full-duplex models in self-play and cross-play and scores both sides with an external judge.

## Evaluation Setup

The abstract reports four tasks, 140 scenarios, 7,560 conversations, and six model pairings.

## Key Limitations

Model-model interaction and external judging are proxies for human dialogue. Dyadic quality is adjacent to proactivity; it does not by itself establish content-grounded reasons to interrupt a person.

## Use For

Use for reciprocal timing, cross-model interaction, and evaluating full-duplex infrastructure separately from useful initiative.
