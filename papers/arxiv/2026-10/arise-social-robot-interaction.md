# From Social Reasoning to Embodied Interaction: An Agentic Framework for Social Robots

[Primary source](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.05964) · Checked 2026-10-08 · arXiv preprint. Review depth: abstract and arXiv metadata; no experiments rerun.

## Why It Matters

Social robots must connect reasoning about people to timely speech and embodied expression, rather than remain a text-only conversational planner.

## Proactivity Signal

ARISE combines multimodal context and long-term memory to choose when and what to communicate, coordinating speech, gestures, and facial behavior in a streaming robot system.

## Evaluation Setup

The abstract reports real-robot evaluations on Sophia and describes both proactive and reactive social interaction.

## Key Limitations

The abstract provides no numerical effect sizes. A demonstrated robot integration does not establish general social competence, household autonomy, or personalized long-term benefit.

## Use For

Use for the perception-memory-social-reasoning-to-action pipeline and separating robot runtime integration from intervention quality.
