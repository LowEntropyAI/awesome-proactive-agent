# Event-Driven Proactive Robot Assistance through Vision-Language Reasoning

[Primary source](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.08344) · Checked 2026-10-08 · arXiv preprint. Review depth: abstract and arXiv metadata; no experiments rerun.

## Why It Matters

Robotic assistance can be triggered by a meaningful environmental change rather than a new explicit task command.

## Proactivity Signal

An event monitor produces stabilized pre/post snapshots. A frozen VLM reasons about the change, decides whether help is needed, and selects action primitives with object identifiers.

## Evaluation Setup

Evaluation covers three real-world tabletop scenarios and compares event-driven assistance with an instruction-driven variant.

## Key Limitations

Three tabletop scenarios and a fixed primitive interface do not establish unrestricted physical-world autonomy. Trigger quality, perception error, and safety constraints remain separate concerns.

## Use For

Use for event-to-intent-to-action pipelines, bounded robot assistance, and comparing unsolicited with instructed actions.
