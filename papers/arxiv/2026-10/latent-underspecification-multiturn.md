# Knowing When Not to Answer: Cross-Domain and Multi-Turn Generalization of Latent Underspecification Signals

[Primary source](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.08413) · Checked 2026-10-08 · arXiv preprint; under review. Review depth: abstract and arXiv metadata; no experiments rerun.

## Why It Matters

A signal that detects an unanswerable single-turn prompt may fail to determine readiness after several clarification turns.

## Proactivity Signal

Hidden-state probes estimate underspecification and gate answering, with cross-domain and multi-turn transfer tests.

## Evaluation Setup

Evaluation uses six datasets and six open language models, plus 423 conversations containing 1,661 turn states. The abstract reports failed zero-shot readiness transfer and no advantage of the tested gate over vanilla or prompt-consolidation baselines.

## Key Limitations

Negative results concern the studied probes and protocols, not proof that all latent readiness policies fail. The arXiv record says under review, not accepted.

## Use For

Use for readiness generalization tests, strong simple baselines, and avoiding single-turn-to-interactive extrapolation.
