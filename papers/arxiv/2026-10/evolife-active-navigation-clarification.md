# Ask to Evolve: A Unified Harness for Lifelong Embodied Navigation with Hybrid Action Space and Persistent Memory

[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.39446) · [Project](https://kai-sheng-caesar.github.io/EvoLife-Web/) · First posted 2026-10-29 · Checked 2026-10-31. Review: primary method/protocol and linked artifacts; no rerun.

## Why It Matters

Adds agent-initiated clarification to navigation instead of silently committing to an uncertain route or target.

## Proactivity Signal

A hybrid movement interface is coupled with active interaction and persistent spatial memory. The agent asks for help or disambiguation, then binds useful observations and dialogue to places.

## Evaluation Setup

Eight benchmarks span vision-language, object, interactive-instance and continuous multi-task navigation. The paper also presents multi-day physical navigation. Report interaction count, success and revisit efficiency together.

## Key Limitations

Navigation goals and available feedback constrain the task. High success with assistance is not unassisted navigation; limited benchmark episodes and physical demonstrations do not establish general lifelong autonomy.

## Use For

Embodied help-seeking and memory reuse, adjacent to proactive user assistance rather than unsolicited service initiation. The inspected project page links the paper but no public implementation, checkpoint or new dataset release was verified.
