# Learning to Clarify Underspecified Intents Under Limited Interaction

[Primary source](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.04719) · Checked 2026-10-08 · arXiv preprint. Review depth: abstract and arXiv metadata; no experiments rerun.

## Why It Matters

Clarification is useful only if the information recovered is worth the user's time and interaction budget.

## Proactivity Signal

A value-of-information-oriented RL policy learns to ask targeted questions with a simulated user, optimizing recovery of intended image-generation outcomes.

## Evaluation Setup

A preregistered human study contains 76 participants and 456 sessions. The abstract reports better reference-image matching with fewer questions, less time, and lower interaction cost.

## Key Limitations

Reference-image recovery is a concrete but narrow intent proxy. Simulator training and limited interaction sessions do not establish durable personalization or utility in unrelated domains.

## Use For

Use for clarification reward design, human validation of simulator-trained policies, and the benefit-versus-burden trade-off.
