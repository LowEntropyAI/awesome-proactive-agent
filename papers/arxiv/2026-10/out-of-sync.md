# Out of sync: Accommodation, evaluation, and emergence in five weeks with an agentic AI teammate

[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.41057) · First posted 2026-10-29 · Checked 2026-11-02. Primary paper and available artifact listings reviewed; no rerun.

## Why It Matters

A user need need not take the form of a question: quick agreement or an overlooked contribution can make intervention useful.

## Proactivity Signal

The teammate joins a group chat on a system trigger to challenge premature consensus or invite a quieter student to speak.

## Evaluation Setup

Seventeen undergraduate teams were assigned to one of two roles; 48 students consented to data use. Discourse, surveys and spoken reflections tracked change over repeated sessions.

## Key Limitations

Helpfulness ratings and perceived collaboration did not move together. Both conditions had an AI teammate, so this is not a comparison with no AI. The manuscript reports submission, not journal acceptance.

## Use For

Study how group norms and reactions to unsolicited interventions change over time.
