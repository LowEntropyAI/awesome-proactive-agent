# Learning to Interact and Direct: Co-Evolving Video Director Agents

[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.41865) · [Project](https://huggingface.co/LIT-review/LIT) · First posted 2026-10-30 · Checked 2026-11-02. Primary paper and available artifact listings reviewed; no rerun.

## Why It Matters

Clarifying a creative preference before generating assets may prevent costly rework later in a production pipeline.

## Proactivity Signal

Within an assigned video brief, the director asks a simulated user to resolve creative choices before spending effort on production.

## Evaluation Setup

The paper compares interaction and production scores on test and out-of-distribution splits, including ablations of the co-evolution setup.

## Key Limitations

User interaction is simulated. Self-directed improvement by the meta engineer is separate from user-facing clarification, and video generation is not streaming assistance.

## Use For

Study intent elicitation within a delegated creative workflow.
