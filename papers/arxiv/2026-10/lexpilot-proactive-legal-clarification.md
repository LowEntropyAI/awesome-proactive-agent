# LexPilot: Benchmarking the Proactive Interaction Ability of Legal LLMs with Laypeople in Chinese Civil Case Consultation

[Primary source](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.23141) · Checked 2026-10-19 · arXiv 2610. Review depth: abstract and arXiv metadata; no experiments rerun.

## Why It Matters

Adjacent active clarification: legal consultation from incomplete layperson descriptions requires eliciting decision-relevant facts.

## Proactivity Signal

LexPilot connects proactive questioning to downstream claim-level verdict prediction instead of evaluating conversational style alone.

## Evaluation Setup

The expert-reviewed benchmark derives complete and underspecified consultation histories from 432 Chinese civil judgments across 36 causes of action.

## Key Limitations

Verdict prediction and retrospective judgment-derived histories do not establish accurate legal advice or real consultation utility. This is domain-specific elicitation, not independent task discovery.

## Use For

Use for missing-fact elicitation and downstream-outcome evaluation. Public dataset files were visible on October 19.
