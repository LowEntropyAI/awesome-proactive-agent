# DIVE: Benchmarking Full-Duplex Interaction with Visual Engagement in Continuous Streams

[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.28405) · First posted 2026-10-20 · Checked 2026-10-24. Review: taxonomy, protocol and limitations; no rerun.

## Why It Matters

The proactive-intervention subset separates noticing an emerging cue from delivering a useful response within its acceptable window.

## Proactivity Signal

Video Timely Intervention and Speech Proactive Intervention test initiating a contribution from visual or semantic cues. Other tasks cover continuous engagement and interaction coordination; not every full-duplex task demonstrates proactivity.

## Evaluation Setup

525 audiovisual samples span six tasks. Participation decisions, response quality, window response rates and reaction latency are reported separately. Sub-second mean latency can coexist with frequent window misses.

## Key Limitations

Short sessions and verbal responses do not establish long-horizon memory, proactive tool use or embodied autonomy. Report proactive-subset scores rather than treating the entire benchmark as an initiative metric.

## Use For

Testing content-grounded intervention and timing under continuous input. Public code, benchmark files and a project-specific checkpoint were not verified in the inspected primary source.
