# Adaptive Personalization Under Preference Epistemic Uncertainty

[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.40930) · [Project](https://github.com/priyanka-dey/adaptive-personalization) · First posted 2026-10-29 · Checked 2026-11-02. Primary paper and available artifact listings reviewed; no rerun.

## Why It Matters

Remembering a preference can lead to the wrong intervention if the evidence for that preference has changed.

## Proactivity Signal

Tests whether the assistant asks or confirms when an old preference becomes uncertain, instead of simply using it again.

## Evaluation Setup

A human preference study grounds strategy labels. Experiments with eight models span 250 personas, five languages and 25,000 dialogue turns, separating strategy selection from free generation.

## Key Limitations

This evaluates response strategies in conversation, not always-on preference sensing. Human strategy judgments do not establish long-term user benefit.

## Use For

Test when personalization should switch from acting on prior knowledge to asking again.
