# Let Me Clarify: Interactive Modeling of Soft Attributes

[Primary source](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.20227) · Checked 2026-10-19 · arXiv 2610. Review depth: abstract and arXiv metadata; no experiments rerun.

## Why It Matters

Adjacent mixed initiative: subjective descriptions can require clarification before they become usable preference attributes.

## Proactivity Signal

AttriFy estimates interpretive softness to guide questions and decide when to generate executable Python attribute functions. Users inspect and refine the resulting interpretations.

## Evaluation Setup

An exploratory within-subjects study with 32 participants compares softness-guided clarification with an LLM baseline.

## Key Limitations

This is user-steerable attribute construction within an existing interaction, not unsolicited need discovery or broad assistant deployment.

## Use For

Use for ambiguity-guided clarification and transparent preference elicitation.
