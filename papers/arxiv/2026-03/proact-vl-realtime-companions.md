# Proact-VL: A Proactive VideoLLM for Real-Time AI Companions

[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.03447) · [Code](https://github.com/microsoft/AnthropomorphicIntelligence/tree/main/Proact-VL)

## Why It Matters

Studies live companions where response timing, content, and response quantity jointly matter.

## Proactivity Signal

Commentary and guidance policies select when to respond or remain silent during video input.

## Evaluation Setup

Live Gaming Benchmark covers solo commentary, co-commentary, and user guidance. The repository documents content/timing metrics, model backbones, and evaluation scripts.

## Key Limitations

Gaming instructions define different speech expectations. LLM judgments and these scenarios do not establish broader assistance utility. Concrete model/data links coexist with an outdated TODO block.

## Use For

Real-time commentary, guidance, and timing-versus-content evaluation.
