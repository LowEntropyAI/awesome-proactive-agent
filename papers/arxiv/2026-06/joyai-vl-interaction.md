# JoyAI-VL-Interaction: Real-Time Vision-Language Interaction Intelligence

[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.14777) · [Code](https://github.com/jd-opensource/JoyAI-VL-Interaction)

## Why It Matters

Releases a vision-first interaction model alongside a deployable streaming system.

## Proactivity Signal

The model internally chooses silence, response, or background delegation each second.

## Evaluation Setup

AdaCodec reduces tokens for predictable frames; ASR/TTS, memory, and background reasoning are pluggable. Evaluation includes human preference comparisons across six real-world scenarios; model and interaction-data resources are linked.

## Key Limitations

Scenario-specific human preference is not broad model superiority. Emergent screen guidance does not establish closed-loop GUI execution or a calibrated interruption policy for every user.

## Use For

Vision-triggered interaction, speak/silence/delegate policies, and deployable streaming assistants.
