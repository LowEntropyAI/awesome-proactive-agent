# Working with Agentic 'Teammates': When a New Organizational Actor Collides with the Human Ecosystem of Work

## Why It Matters

This study examines a persistent proactive agent inside real organizational workflows rather than a short laboratory task. It shows that proactive agents change not only individual productivity but also tacit coordination rules, team relationships, trust, and human agency.

## Proactivity Signal

The deployed agent acts as a continuing multi-user teammate that initiates contributions across team workflows instead of responding only to one person's direct request.

## Evaluation Setup

The authors conduct an in-situ qualitative study across multiple teams in a large technology company. They analyze breakdowns and negotiations around tacit workflow rules, the relational boundaries of a non-human teammate, and redistribution of trust and agency.

## Key Limitations

The qualitative setting limits causal attribution and generalization across organizations or agent designs. Public evidence does not isolate model behavior from deployment policy, organizational incentives, or novelty effects.

## Use For

Use this for real-world proactive-agent deployment, multi-user workplace agents, organizational human factors, trust redistribution, and governance of persistent agent teammates.
