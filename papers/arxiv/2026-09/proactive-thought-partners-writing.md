# Designing Proactive Thought Partners for Writing

## Why It Matters

This paper studies proactive support above the autocomplete level: configurable partners can offer ideation, reflection, and revision help as a writer's cognitive needs change. It contributes situated design evidence rather than only a generation benchmark.

## Proactivity Signal

Writers configure each partner's role and timing conditions in advance. During writing, relevant partners initiate suggestions at those moments without a new prompt, while the writer can ignore them, use them as inspiration, or ask the system to apply a change.

## Evaluation Setup

The technology probe was deployed with 16 participants for one week, followed by diary collection and semi-structured interviews. Participants used proactive suggestions for idea generation and self-monitoring and preferred lightweight visual presentation and non-directive rhetorical framing when interventions might disrupt flow.

## Key Limitations

The study is small, short, and centered on a configurable research probe rather than a production writing system. Because users define timing conditions prospectively, the results do not establish whether an autonomous policy can infer the right intervention moment from behavior alone.

## Use For

Use this for proactive writing assistants, user-configurable trigger policies, cognitive-level support, non-intrusive presentation, and studies that treat ignored suggestions as meaningful interaction evidence.
