# Foundations of Proactive Agents: Principles, Technical Layers, and Proactivity-Gym

## Why It Matters

This work frames proactive agents around three coupled objectives: completing useful work, allocating compute at appropriate times, and preserving user trust. It makes temporal resource allocation part of proactivity rather than only a systems concern.

## Proactivity Signal

An agent anticipates needs, chooses an activation trigger and intervention depth, schedules work around resource availability and user focus, and must account for how each intervention changes future trust.

## Evaluation Setup

The paper proposes a five-dimension design space and PROACTIVITY-GYM, a simulation testbed with multi-day scenarios, stateful environments, and persona-conditioned users. Evaluation across 23 model-harness configurations exposes gaps across the three objectives; a 30-participant study finds sharp trust declines after misaligned interventions and a preference for sleep-time assistance.

## Key Limitations

The framework is broad and its simulated users cannot establish deployment validity. The human study is small, and the three objectives still require application-specific weights and authorization rules.

## Use For

Use this for proactive-agent definitions, multi-day evaluation, compute scheduling, trust-aware intervention design, and separating task capability from temporal allocation and user trust.
