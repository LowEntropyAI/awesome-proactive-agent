# Safety Nudges: User-Facing Interventions for Real-Time AI Risk Awareness

## Why It Matters

Safety Nudges adds a proactive safety sidecar to ordinary chatbot use. Instead of relying only on model-level safeguards, it surfaces lightweight warnings when the ongoing conversation exhibits risks that users may not notice themselves.

## Proactivity Signal

The browser tool monitors chatbot conversations, detects concerning behavior such as hallucination, sycophancy, overconfidence, or anthropomorphism, and initiates an in-situ warning without the user asking for a safety review.

## Evaluation Setup

A two-week field study with 45 frequent chatbot users combines interaction logs, surveys, and per-nudge feedback. Participants generally found the warnings clear, useful, and minimally disruptive, and nearly all reported greater awareness, although awareness did not reliably translate into observable behavior change.

## Key Limitations

The study is short and measures awareness more clearly than durable decision improvement. Detection calibration, false-alert fatigue, generalization across chatbot interfaces, and the possibility of over-trusting the warning system remain open.

## Use For

Use this for proactive safety interfaces, real-time risk warnings, sidecar agents, intervention calibration, and separating awareness gains from behavioral outcomes.
