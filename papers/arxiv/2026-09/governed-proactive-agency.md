# When Intelligence Becomes Agency: A Theory of Governed, Proactive Agency for Symbiotic AI Systems

## Why It Matters

This paper treats activation as a distinct problem: capable persistent assistants still need a principled way to decide whether any behavior is warranted. It connects initiative to continuing authorization, accountability, personalization, and deliberate restraint.

## Proactivity Signal

The framework models behavior selection among acting, asking, monitoring, deferring, and refraining under a standing but revocable mandate. Evidence can accumulate while the system remains behaviorally silent until an activation threshold is met.

## Evaluation Setup

The work is conceptual rather than empirical. It supplies a formal vocabulary, agency signatures, an evaluation framework, illustrative benchmark scenarios, and a reference layer stack for assessing capability, authorization, timing, traceability, restraint, and adaptation.

## Key Limitations

The proposed measures and scenarios are not validated through a released benchmark, trained policy, or user deployment. Several constructs would require operational definitions and reliable access to user state before they could support reproducible comparison.

## Use For

Use this for defining activation gates, separating autonomy from delegated agency, designing revocable mandates, auditing restraint, and extending proactive-agent benchmarks beyond task success.
