# Oops, Not Now: PEARL, a RAG-Based Support Agent for Gameplay and What Players Want from AI Help

## Why It Matters

PEARL is valuable as a negative-result design probe: technically grounded proactive help can still reduce usefulness when it arrives at the wrong time or in an unwanted form. It gives concrete user evidence against equating context awareness with acceptable intervention.

## Proactivity Signal

The gameplay assistant uses natural-language and board-state retrieval to push contextual scaffolding during play, including assistance the user did not explicitly request at that moment.

## Evaluation Setup

A qualitative study with 10 participants compares PEARL with a community-based visualization system. Five participants minimized or abandoned the AI tool; proactive delivery, generic responses, and trust deficits were recurring causes, while four found the grounded explanations useful in contexts where they initiated interaction.

## Key Limitations

The study is small and tied to one parallel-programming puzzle game. It does not isolate timing from content quality experimentally, and the system lacks a learned help-or-silence policy.

## Use For

Use this for negative evidence on proactive assistance, user-controlled invocation, educational game agents, interruption-cost design, and measuring abandonment rather than only answer quality.
