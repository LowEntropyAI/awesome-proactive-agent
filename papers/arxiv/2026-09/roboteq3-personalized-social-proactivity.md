# RobotEQ 3.0: Towards Personalized Social Proactive Intelligence in Embodied Agents

## Why It Matters

RobotEQ 3.0 moves proactive embodied assistance beyond an average-user standard by linking individual traits to preferred robot actions in social situations.

## Proactivity Signal

Given a social context and user profile, the agent predicts which proactive action that specific person would prefer before the person explicitly requests it.

## Evaluation Setup

Participants complete a structured profile covering factors correlated with expectations of embodied agents and choose preferred actions from candidates. The benchmark evaluates personalized action prediction and reports substantial inter-annotator variation, with user traits improving personalized prediction.

## Key Limitations

Candidate-action preference prediction does not test autonomous execution, timing, or recovery after an unwanted intervention. Profile features can correlate with preferences without providing a causal or stable model of a person.

## Use For

Use this for personalized embodied assistance, social-action preference modeling, user-conditioned proactive evaluation, and studying disagreement hidden by population-average labels.
