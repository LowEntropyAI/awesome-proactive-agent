# Visual Proactivity: Enhancing Human-Robot Collaboration Through Intent Communication

[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.21729) · First posted 2026-09-18 · Checked 2026-11-01. Review: primary method/protocol and linked artifacts; no rerun.

## Why It Matters

Separates reactive, anticipatory and proactive robot behavior and tests whether people perceive the difference, in a physical task with no verbal request.

## Proactivity Signal

The robot sets its goal by blending the predicted human goal with the closest goal, so its visible motion can influence where the person goes. If the person keeps deviating, it slows and asks verbally. Here proactive means actively influencing human decisions.

## Evaluation Setup

A single-blind, within-subject lab study with 30 participants, 4 goals and 3 behaviors (12 trials each), using 7-point scales and interviews. Proactive behavior was distinguished from reactive (p < 0.001). The preference hypothesis was not fully confirmed: most of its items were not significant.

## Key Limitations

One participant at a time in a controlled lab, covering only the approach phase of handover. The blending threshold was not ablated. Proactive trials took 8.3 seconds longer on average. The robot is influencing a route in a fixed task, not discovering an unstated need.

## Use For

Human-factors evidence for robot initiative conveyed through motion. The venue comes from the arXiv comment; no code or data link was found and nothing was reproduced.
