# DocuTeam: Mixed-Initiative Multi-Agent Discussions around Evolving Documents

## Why It Matters

DocuTeam makes document changes an event stream that can trigger collaborative discussion. It reduces the burden on users to repeatedly summon and orchestrate multiple agents while preserving their ability to redirect or ignore the discussion.

## Proactivity Signal

Agents monitor an evolving shared document, proactively start or redirect discussions when changes create a useful opening, and surface concerns or alternatives without waiting for an explicit prompt.

## Evaluation Setup

A within-subjects study with 20 participants compares DocuTeam against a non-proactive baseline. Outputs produced with DocuTeam are rated more novel, relevant, and specific without a reported increase in cognitive load, and interaction traces show document changes prompting iterative agent-user refinement loops.

## Key Limitations

The study is small and focused on open-ended document work. It does not yet isolate which trigger policies minimize unwanted interruptions or establish long-term effects on authorship, ownership, and reliance.

## Use For

Use this for mixed-initiative document agents, event-triggered discussion, multi-agent collaboration, proactive critique, and human control over agent-initiated conversations.
