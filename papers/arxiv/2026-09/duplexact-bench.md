# DuplexAct-Bench: Broadening Full-Duplex Speech Evaluation toward Proactive Interaction across Diverse Behavioral Requirements

## Why It Matters

DuplexAct-Bench evaluates a wider behavior set than ordinary turn-taking benchmarks, including proactive initiation and deliberate silence alongside interruption, yielding, and backchanneling.

## Proactivity Signal

A full-duplex system must decide when, whether, and how to participate across pre-session, in-session, and no-explicit-request conditions, including choosing active silence.

## Evaluation Setup

The bilingual benchmark contains 1,290 English and Chinese streaming trials covering six behaviors. Twelve full-duplex speech systems are evaluated separately on timing and content, revealing frequent mismatches between semantically good responses and appropriate participation timing.

## Key Limitations

The benchmark uses scripted streaming trials rather than long-term relationships or personalized interruption preferences. Coverage across six behaviors is broad, but deployment costs and downstream task benefit remain outside the reported evaluation.

## Use For

Use this for proactive voice-agent evaluation, active silence, interruption and yielding, bilingual full-duplex testing, and separating content quality from timing quality.
