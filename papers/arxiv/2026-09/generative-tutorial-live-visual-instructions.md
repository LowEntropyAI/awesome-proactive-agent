# Generative Tutorial: Towards Live Contextualized Visual Instructions for Physical Tasks

## Why It Matters

Generative Tutorial moves visual guidance from static, pre-authored instructions into the user's current workspace and task flow. It studies whether proactive generation can reduce the mental translation between a tutorial's context and the user's physical environment.

## Proactivity Signal

The system observes the workspace and predicted outcomes of preceding actions, then generates contextual goal images and demonstration videos as the task unfolds instead of waiting for a fresh instruction request at every step.

## Evaluation Setup

A formative evaluation examines current image and video generation across 15 physical tasks. A 24-participant lab study compares the augmented-reality prototype with pre-authored guidance and reports higher task-output quality, greater perceived workspace correspondence, and shorter step-confirmation intervals.

## Key Limitations

The study is bounded to laboratory tasks, and generation errors can create misleading physical guidance. The system does not yet learn a calibrated help-or-silence policy or validate safety in high-risk tasks.

## Use For

Use this for proactive AR guidance, live visual instruction, context-conditioned generation, physical-task assistance, and studying trust under generative errors.
