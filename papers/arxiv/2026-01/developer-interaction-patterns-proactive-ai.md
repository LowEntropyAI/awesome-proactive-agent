# Developer Interaction Patterns with Proactive AI: A Five-Day Field Study

## Why It Matters

This field study grounds proactive coding-assistant design in real developer behavior rather than lab-only task completion.

## Proactivity Signal

The agent proactively surfaces code-quality suggestions, and the study analyzes when developers accept, ignore, or experience those suggestions as disruption.

## Evaluation Setup

The paper observes developers over five days in an IDE setting and analyzes interaction timing, workflow phase, acceptance, and perceived value.

## Key Limitations

The deployment period is still short, and the study focuses on coding rather than broader computer-use workflows.

## Use For

Use this for evidence about workflow boundaries, timing calibration, and real-world acceptance of proactive developer tools.
