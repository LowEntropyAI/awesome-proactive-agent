# From Autonomous Agents to Cognitive Symbionts: Toward AI-Augmented Human Intelligence

[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.05376) · First posted 2026-11-03 · Checked 2026-11-06. Primary paper and available artifact listings reviewed; no rerun.

## Why It Matters

Frames proactive assistance as augmenting one person’s own intelligence, with silence treated as a deliberate output rather than a failure to respond.

## Proactivity Signal

The proposed companion chooses at each moment among silence, a hint, a suggestion, a question or acting on the person’s behalf, with confidence thresholds that rise with how intrusive the help is.

## Evaluation Setup

None. A 59-page position paper with no algorithm, prototype or measured results; its figures and tables are conceptual.

## Key Limitations

The thresholds and policies are not operationalized or tested. The paper flags its own risks, including covert help in exams and conversations and bystander privacy. A pilot mentioned on the project page is not reported in the paper.

## Use For

Vocabulary for graded intervention and silence when framing a wearable proactive companion.
