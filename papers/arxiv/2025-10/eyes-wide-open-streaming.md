# Eyes Wide Open: Ego Proactive Video-LLM for Streaming Video

[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.14560) · [Code](https://github.com/SooLab/EyeWO)

## Why It Matters

Connects egocentric streaming perception to coherent, timely answers rather than only offline clip QA.

## Proactivity Signal

The assistant chooses when to answer evolving questions as the video unfolds.

## Evaluation Setup

ESTP-Bench and ESTP-F1 evaluate coherence, responsiveness, and efficiency. A data engine, multi-stage training, and dynamic compression support the model; online/offline baselines are compared.

## Key Limitations

Query-conditioned timing does not establish independent need discovery. Repository setup includes placeholders, so documentation availability is not an installation receipt.

## Use For

Egocentric answer timing, streaming compression, and ESTP evaluation.
