# Vinci2: Providing Proactive Assistance in Continuous Egocentric Videos

## Why It Matters

Vinci2 makes proactive egocentric assistance a when-and-whether decision over continuous first-person video, rather than answering a query or firing on every detected event. EgoServe also connects immediate safety alerts with cross-session habit coaching in one benchmark.

## Proactivity Signal

EgoMemo continuously builds multi-scale temporal summaries, a semantic knowledge graph, and a visual archive. At each timestep it retrieves relevant evidence, decides whether assistance is warranted, remains silent when it is not, and generates a grounded intervention when it is.

## Evaluation Setup

EgoServe contains more than 3,000 service instances across 10 categories and four temporal horizons, from instant alerts to multi-day routine optimization. The training-free EgoMemo establishes a benchmark baseline and is also tested on five existing egocentric-video benchmarks.

## Key Limitations

EgoServe is derived from existing recorded datasets and semi-automated annotations rather than live user requests and acceptance feedback. Reported overall F1 remains low, showing that open-ended long-horizon triggering is far from solved.

## Use For

Use this for egocentric proactive agents, when-and-whether intervention policies, long-term wearable memory, safety alerts, habit coaching, and evaluating silence alongside assistance.
