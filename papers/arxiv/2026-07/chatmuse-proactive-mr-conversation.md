# ChatMuse: Supporting In-Person Small-Group Conversation Experience with a Proactive Assistive AI Agent in Mixed Reality

## Why It Matters

ChatMuse studies proactive assistance inside a live multi-person conversation, where poorly timed guidance can distract the wearer or disrupt social ownership. It combines verbal and nonverbal cues with feedback from the user's subsequent behavior.

## Proactivity Signal

The MR agent monitors all participants, decides when conversational guidance is useful, and renders real-time suggestions about the wearer's verbal and nonverbal behavior without waiting for a request. Later user behavior is fed back to improve subsequent support.

## Evaluation Setup

The UIST 2026 work uses within-subject studies and focus groups with six participant groups totaling 18 people. Participants reported benefits for engagement and contribution, while also identifying both unnecessary interventions and missed opportunities.

## Key Limitations

The study uses a bulky Meta Quest Pro, limited nonverbal sensing, three-to-four-person groups, designated topics, and conversations of roughly 15 minutes. Some users felt overwhelmed, and the current system does not yet personalize agency or guarantee conversation ownership over long-term use.

## Use For

Use this for proactive MR interfaces, small-group conversation support, multimodal social cues, user studies of intervention timing, and designs that preserve human agency while offering suggestions.
