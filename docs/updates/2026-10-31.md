## WakeGate classification and products

[WakeGate source snapshot](https://github.com/MIAOMIAO-owo/wake-gate/tree/82fffd3ae0e4404f6b4a33ae05986c4318deb962) supports a **conditional wake-up component**, not a standalone persistent runtime. External scheduling runs the model-free judge; a host-provided HTTP endpoint must resume the session; the agent chooses whether to help. Moved the entry to Supporting Components and retained upstream attribution.

Source corrections: the crisis flag bypasses judge conditions but not the separate cooldown; missing conversation time can request a wake; `gather()` remains placeholders. Event listeners, promise/forced exceptions, hourly/daily caps and ignored-message backoff are not wired in the inspected scripts. `action` and `dry_run` are state fields, not enforced no-send controls. No upstream code was executed or changed.

Added [Meta Muse](https://about.fb.com/news/2026/09/introducing-muse-personal-ai-agent/), [Manus Cue](https://www.manus.im/blog/introducing-manus-2-0) and [Grok Bot](https://docs.x.ai/grok-bot/overview) to Product Reference, with official links and explicit evidence limits. Muse's standing-goal work differs from its underlying Muse Spark model; Cue's agent identities/delegation do not establish autonomous need discovery or parity with Manus Automations; Grok Bot's [routines](https://docs.x.ai/grok-bot/skills-routines-and-automations) establish configured scheduled/event activation, not general unsolicited initiative.
