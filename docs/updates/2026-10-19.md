## Streaming and embodied findings

- **ProPerAgent / ProPerMem:** personal evidence must justify speaking without a query. This differs from the generic memory-QA papers removed earlier. Separate windowed and persistent streaming programs, and do not move scores between protocols.
- **P3I-Assistant-4B / P3IBench:** a trained streaming baseline and evaluation of perception, hazard prediction, and intervention. Monitoring and goal-conditioned guidance are not identical tasks; use subset scores rather than a single autonomy claim.
- **SurgStreamer:** structured-state reasoning for requested event alerts. Gold monitoring conditions, retrospective tests, and absent prospective clinical validation limit interpretation.
- **PAM:** reflected finite-state routine memory controls proactive task selection versus NO_ACT. Small desk routines and false-assistance trade-offs remain important limits.

These findings suggest more explicit evaluation of whether evidence justifies interruption, alongside content and timing. This is an inference across the inspected papers, not a field-wide trend estimate.
