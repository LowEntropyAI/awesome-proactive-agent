## Added papers

| First posted | Work | Role | Scope and reason to include |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sep 24 | [Risk-Adaptive Programming Feedback](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.29874) | Core | Calibrated risk and a capacity budget decide when to intervene after failed submissions. Offline replay only, with no learner study. |
| Sep 23 | [Talk2Escape](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.28296) | Adjacent | A navigation agent asks for help when kinematic triggers detect looping or drift. Help-seeking for its own task. |
| Sep 18 | [RobotEQ-Video](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.21371) | Core | Benchmark for judging candidate robot actions from video, with a world-state taxonomy. Adds one benchmark row. |
| Sep 18 | [PV-Care](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.22024) | Core | EEG state triggers voice help, and resting triggers none. Validated on healthy participants and simulated scenarios, not MCI field use. |
| Sep 18 | [Visual Proactivity](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.21729) | Core | A 30-person handover study comparing reactive, anticipatory and proactive robot motion. Preference results were mixed. |

All five have the repository's five-section evidence notes and attributed original figures. The role labels are editorial judgments from those notes. Visual Proactivity is the closest call: it influences a route in a fixed task rather than discovering an unstated need.
