# November 2 literature scan

Since the [November 1 sweep](2026-11-01.md), 473 arXiv records were returned across cs.AI, cs.CL, cs.CV, cs.HC, cs.RO, cs.MA and cs.LG for the submission window October 29, 18:00 UTC through November 2 (scan date in Singapore). The latest returned timestamp was October 30, 17:59:59 UTC. This is what was public at scan time, not a claim about papers still awaiting announcement.

We screened titles and abstracts for initiative, clarification, intervention, contextual verification and preference uncertainty, compared candidates with the README, and inspected the included papers and accessible author artifacts. A supplementary October title search helped check overlap. The date below is v1 submission, not the day the paper was added to the Atlas. All six are recorded as preprints; no experiments or artifacts were run.

| First submitted | Paper | Inclusion boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Oct 30 | [BlindToChange](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.41435) | Adjacent benchmark: look for an unmentioned change within a delegated recommendation task. Public scripts and probe files. |
| Oct 30 | [When Should AI Agents Ask?](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.41129) | Adjacent: coordinate clarification across parallel agents sharing one person's attention. Synthetic schedules and an LLM case study. |
| Oct 30 | [LIT](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.41865) | Adjacent: questions to a simulated creative client; not a streaming model. The public workflow is distinct from model weights. |
| Oct 29 | [Out of Sync](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.41057) | Core: trigger-initiated contributions during a five-week team study. Data are available on request, not publicly downloadable. |
| Oct 29 | [P-ContextBench](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.40959) | Supporting: the conflict subset tests when to warn or clarify; the whole benchmark is not an initiative evaluation. |
| Oct 29 | [Adaptive Personalization](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.40930) | Adjacent: whether the assistant elicits, confirms or recalibrates instead of reusing a stale preference. The linked repository was README-only. |

PersonaChatBench measures responsiveness to expressed needs; TurnTurk-GPT predicts turn boundaries rather than whether an intervention is warranted. Neither was added. No new streaming assistance model qualified in this sweep. The bibliography, six evidence notes and figure-backed bilingual Recent cards, and three benchmark rows were updated; the essential-reading selection was not changed.
